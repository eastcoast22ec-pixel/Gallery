Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Render Billing Engine V4 handles information when its owner uses Google OAuth and the application's operational workflow.

Information accessed

The application accesses only the Google account permissions that are explicitly requested and displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen. These permissions are used to operate the owner's Google Apps Script source-management and control workflow.

How information is used

  • To read or update the owner's Apps Script project content when the owner authorizes those actions.
  • To support operational control, verification, recovery, and audit records for the owner's workflow.
  • To maintain continuity of the owner's Render billing and service-state monitoring process.

Storage and retention

Operational records may be retained in resources controlled by the owner, including the owner's Google Apps Script project and Google Sheets control surface, when required for continuity, auditability, or recovery. OAuth credentials and secrets are not intentionally published in this website.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties. Information is processed only as necessary to operate the owner's workflow and the connected Google services.

Security

The application is designed to use least-privilege OAuth scopes, controlled operations, and rollback/recovery safeguards. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account security settings.

Deletion and revocation

The owner can revoke the application's Google access from Google Account permissions. Operational records stored in owner-controlled Google resources can be removed by the owner when they are no longer required.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact shown for Render Billing Engine V4 in the Google OAuth consent screen.
